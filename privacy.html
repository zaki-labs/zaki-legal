Privacy Policy

Updated October 29, 2026

Zaki AI LLC ("Zaki," "we," "us") provides the Zaki app and related services. This policy explains how we handle personal information. Contact team@zakilabs.ai with questions or privacy requests.

Information we collect

The information we collect depends on the features you use:

  • Account information: your name, phone number, account identifiers and information used to authenticate your account.
  • Food preferences and activity: food choices, ratings, meal records, order history, dietary preferences and information we infer about your tastes. Allergy and dietary information you provide may reveal health information or religious beliefs.
  • Messages and photos: conversations with Zaki, feedback, selected meal photos and associated photo information, such as creation time when available. Photo access may also let the app display recent photos on your device.
  • Location and delivery information: location when you grant device permission, addresses, delivery instructions and location choices you provide or make available through a connected service.
  • Connected-service information: account and session information needed to maintain a connection, saved addresses, order and cart information, and payment-method references and display details. Payment providers handle payment entry and processing.
  • Technical and usage information: service interactions, identifiers, network and device information, logs, errors and diagnostic records. These records may be associated with your account.

We receive information from you, your use of Zaki, your device and services you choose to connect. Avoid submitting sensitive information unrelated to the features you use.

How we use information

We use information to provide and maintain Zaki, authenticate accounts, personalize suggestions and Flavor DNA, analyze and record meals, support connected-service and ordering features, respond to requests, troubleshoot and improve the service, prevent misuse, manage operations and comply with legal obligations. Personalization may use your preferences, conversations, meals and connected order history, including automated analysis and background updates.

AI processing

Zaki uses third-party AI services provided by OpenAI to support conversations, food suggestions, meal-photo analysis and food tagging. Depending on the feature, information sent for processing may include messages and relevant conversation history, food preferences and dietary information, selected photos, meal and restaurant information, order history, and relevant address or cart context. Analysis may also occur when saved meals or connected order history are updated.

Information sent to an AI provider is processed outside your device. Providers may retain request information under their applicable terms and data-handling policies. See OpenAI's API data controls. Device permissions and acceptance of this policy do not replace any separate permission required for AI data sharing. Contact us about stopping processing or deleting your account.

How we share information

We share information with service providers that support hosting, storage, account authentication, verification messages, AI processing, payments, diagnostics and support, as needed for those functions. When you use a connected service or authorize an order, relevant information is also handled by that service, payment processors, restaurants and delivery participants under their own terms and privacy policies.

We may disclose information to comply with law or legal process, protect rights and safety, investigate misuse, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization or transfer of our business, subject to applicable law. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, considering the information's nature, account activity, operational needs and legal obligations. Removing an item from the app may not immediately remove related records from every system. Following an account-deletion request, some information may remain for security, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution or legal requirements, and in backups until they are replaced or expire. Independent providers may retain their own records.

You can request account deletion through available in-app controls or by emailing team@zakilabs.ai. Contact us if an in-app request cannot be completed. Deleting a Zaki account does not delete accounts or cancel orders or subscriptions with other services.

Your choices and rights

Available app controls let you manage profile details, food preferences, meal records and connected services. You can manage camera, photo-library and location permissions in device settings. Revoking permission or disconnecting a service does not itself erase information already received.

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, or to restrict or object to certain processing. Email team@zakilabs.ai to make a request or appeal a request decision. We may need to verify your identity or an authorized agent's authority. Applicable exceptions and limitations may apply. We do not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Security and processing location

We use safeguards designed to protect personal information, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Zaki is intended for adults in the United States, and information may be processed in the United States and other locations where our providers operate.

Children

Zaki is intended for people who are at least 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

Website and policy changes

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process visitor information under GitHub's Privacy Statement. We do not use personal information for cross-site advertising tracking, and these policy pages do not respond differently to browser Do Not Track signals.

We may update this policy as our services or practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. We provide additional notice or obtain consent when required by applicable law; an update does not override rights or obligations relating to previously collected information.
